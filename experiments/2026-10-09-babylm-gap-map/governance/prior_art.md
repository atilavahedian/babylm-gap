# BabyLM prior-art map

Purpose: label every attack card honestly ("never call a known idea new") and start Phase 3 from the frontier. Compiled 2026-10-09 from the three BabyLM findings papers, their workshop papers, and data-constrained pretraining work outside BabyLM. Numbers come from the papers; "n/r" = not confirmed. Single seed unless stated.

Companion files on babylm-1 (experimenter): `docs/babylm/priors_data_efficiency.md` (exact quotes, compute cost per row) and `docs/babylm/facts_2025.md` (2025 recipes, rules on teachers/synthetic data). This map adds BabyLM 2023-24, the 2025 workshop papers, and the legality and second-size columns.

**Card check protocol.** For each card answer one line: *measured before? closest relative (row), what it got, regime, legal?* A card whose mechanism matches a row is labelled KNOWN; a new combination or a new regime is labelled as such, not as new.

**What the three years say**
- Only objective changes won big and repeated: causal+masked (GPT-BERT, AntLM). Everything else is ±1-3 points at 1 seed, i.e. inside the noise floor.
- Curriculum failed three years running. Linguistic bias hurt. Vision did not help text. Exotic tokenizers lost to BPE.
- 2025 at 10 epochs: best 10M result is +1.0 NLP (1 seed, data-confounded). At 100M, no submission beat the baseline.
- Almost nothing was tested at a second model size against a strong baseline, and almost nobody ran 3 seeds. The one 10-seed study saw 1-3 point effects. **That gap (multi-seed, two sizes, beyond noise) is ours to fill.**
- Prize size: for the best 2023 recipe, 10x data ≈ +5 BLiMP, +0.08 Agg; 2025 GPT-BERT 10M→100M ≈ +5.7 NLP (57.3→63.0).
- Eval traps ([25.16](https://aclanthology.org/2025.babylm-main.16.pdf)): ties count as correct (a NaN model scores 100 on BLiMP); 22 of 67 BLiMP subtasks share words; untrained model ≈78 on adj-nominalisation; a diverged run scored 45.4 aggregate, above all real models. AoA/WUG/human-likeness unreliable. 2024 reused validation as test.

## C. Where the frontier is open (inference, for Phase 3)

- **Measured, legal, and transferable but never tried in BabyLM:** Kim-style heavy weight decay + ensemble→self-distillation at 10 epochs; weight averaging (LAWA/EMA); AR token-noise augmentation (−0.21 nats, but at >10 epochs); multi-seed, two-size tests of anything.
- **Never measured on LMs at all (genuinely open, still label as known-family):** out-of-fold distillation; RHO-LOSS in LM pretraining; nonce-word augmentation's effect on ordinary LM loss/BLiMP; entity anonymisation as pretraining augmentation; Muon on a fixed corpus over many epochs.
- **Tried in BabyLM and dead:** data-order and vocabulary curricula, linguistic bias, vision, exotic tokenizers, external-teacher RL (also illegal).
- **Tried, small, inside noise:** adaptive masking, distillation (+1-3), contrastive synthetic data, MTP, ELC layer mixing.
- **Only big repeated win:** the causal+masked objective, already in the GPT-BERT baseline. Beating it needs a gain on top of it, at ≤10 epochs, where the outside literature says most regularisation tricks have not yet started to pay.
