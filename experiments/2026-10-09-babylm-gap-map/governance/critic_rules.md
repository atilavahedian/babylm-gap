# BabyLM critic rules (registered before any result)

Mandate: Atila's BabyLM brief, THE JUDGE section. Critic registers rules before results, audits every claimed win, and can veto. Prior rulings on sprint-1 and research/pi-eval-1en95q are background only. Every ruling is a timestamped row in `critic_log.md`; the commit time counts.

Sources checked 2026-10-09: BabyLM 2025 call (arXiv 2502.10645), Findings of the Third BabyLM Challenge (2025.babylm-main.28, Table 3), official pipeline `babylm/evaluation-pipeline-2025` @ `ref-0243` (2025-08-19), Păpușoi & Nisioi 2025 (2025.babylm-main.16).

## R0. Track limits (binding; a violation is out-of-track, never a win)

1. Unique training text <= 10M whitespace-separated words (strict-small) or <= 100M (strict).
2. Total exposure <= 100M words (strict-small) / 1B (strict), counting every pass, i.e. <= 10 epochs of the full corpus. Exposure = whitespace words fed as training input, summed over all passes and all objectives. One sequence used twice in a step (two views, two objectives on separate forwards) counts twice.
3. Every model trained anywhere in the pipeline (teacher, tokenizer-free helper, reward model, generator) shares the same budget: the sum of text seen by all training must stay within the limits. Synthetic text generated by a model trained on the corpus is allowed and counts toward exposure.
4. No external pretrained weights, embeddings, tokenizers, taggers, LLM judges or LLM-generated text. Tokenizers are trained only on the track's training corpus.
5. Each run logs a word-exposure counter and saves checkpoints at 1M..10M (every 1M) and 10M..100M (every 10M) words.

## R1. The ruler

- **Pipeline certification:** before training anything, the runner evaluates the official released checkpoints (`BabyLM-community/babylm-baseline-10m-gpt-bert-*` and GPT-2 10M) with our pipeline install. Zero-shot scores must match the published per-task numbers (leaderboard or model card) within 0.5 points on zero-shot tasks; otherwise the pipeline is not certified and no score counts. Record pipeline commit, data-zip hashes (EWoK password zip included), Python/torch versions.
- **Reproduction pass:** a reproduced recipe passes when its 3-seed mean is within max(2 x floor, 1.5 points) of the published Table 3 value on NLP score and on macro average, using seeds 0, 1, 2 (all runs reported; no reseeding). A failed reproduction is diagnosed before Phase 2; it is not silently replaced by the released checkpoint.
- **Published 10M targets (Findings Table 3):** GPT-BERT mixed-MNTP NLP 57.3 / macro 35.4; masked-MNTP 57.3 / 33.4; causal-MNTP 56.7 / 37.1; GPT-2 49.1 / 34.5; NLP winner AMLM-Hard-Decay 58.3 / 33.3; human-likeness winner MoEP 31.5 HL / 42.3 macro.
- **Reference ("the reproduced winning recipe"):** whichever reproduced recipe has the highest 3-seed mean on the primary metric, not the named winner. Note: the 2025 NLP winner beat the baseline by about 1 point, likely inside noise; if it does not reproduce above GPT-BERT, GPT-BERT is the reference.
- **Sprint-1 port:** a label row, not a reference candidate unless it beats the others.
- **Noise floor:** per metric, pooled per-seed SD across all reproduced recipes (3 seeds each, pooled df). The critic registers the numbers in `critic_log.md` once the ruler lands; they are frozen afterwards.

## R2. Metrics

- **Primary:** official 2025 NLP score (full zero-shot + finetuning), computed by the official collation/leaderboard code at the certified commit, with the official fine-tuning hyperparameters, identical for reference and attack.
- **Co-primary:** held-out loss = causal next-token loss in **bits per whitespace word** on the BabyLM test split, so tokenizer changes cannot game it. A model without a causal mode cannot claim this leg.
- **Reported, not a win criterion:** macro average and human-likeness (seed-to-seed swings there are large: AMLM-Hard 26.7 vs AMLM-Hard-Decay 8.4).
- **Tie rule (known pipeline artifact):** `compute_results.py` picks `torch.max(...)[1]`, so a tie picks index 0; a model scoring both sentences equally can be counted correct (Păpușoi & Nisioi 2025). Every zero-shot result reports the tie count per task, and every claimed win must also hold with ties scored as 0.5.

## R3. Selection discipline

- Screens select on the dev split loss and the official **fast** suite only. The test split is touched only at promotion.
- The fast suite is a subsample of the full suite. A claimed win must also hold on **full-minus-fast** items (never used in selection), not only on the full total.
- Tuning budgets are logged per arm. The reference gets at least the same number of tuning trials as the attack, on the same dev data.

## R4. The win bar

All of these, or no win:
1. 3 seeds (0, 1, 2) of the attack and of the reference, same epochs (<= 10), data, tokenizer policy, eval revision and fine-tuning settings.
2. Attack mean minus reference mean > 2 x floor on the primary metric and on held-out bits per word (lower), with no other registered composite dropping by more than 2 x floor.
3. Holds at two model sizes at least 3x apart in non-embedding parameters. Clearing the bar at both sizes = "holding" (or "growing" if the larger gain is bigger). Clearing at one size only = "fades at second size", reported as such, not a win.
4. Compute: training FLOPs reported for every arm. If an attack uses more than 1.25x the reference's FLOPs, it must also beat a FLOP-matched reference (wider or deeper model, same epochs).
5. Passes the R5 audit.

## R5. Audit for every claimed win

- Leakage: 13-gram overlap of all training text (including synthetic) against every eval set (BLiMP, supplement, EWoK, entity tracking, WUG, COMPS, reading, AoA C4 set, GLUE splits); report counts, and any non-trivial overlap voids the claim until explained.
- Word accounting: exposure counter and checkpoint word counts verified from logs, including teacher/generator training.
- No pretrained-weight or external-model contamination (R0.4).
- Fair comparison: R3 tuning parity, R4.4 compute parity, same seeds, all runs reported.
- Determinism: scores reproduce from the saved checkpoint with the certified pipeline.

## R6. Attack cards

- Each card (mechanism, why it closes its bottleneck, null twin, kill rule, prior-art line) is committed before its first run; the critic logs acceptance. A result committed before its card's acceptance is a label, not a screen.
- Null twin: same compute, exposure, tuning budget and code path, with the mechanism's information removed (e.g. shuffled/random signal), not just switched off.
- Kill rule: numeric, on dev bits per word and/or the fast suite, with a stated budget point (e.g. at 2 epochs). The critic may tighten a kill rule before the run, never after.
- Novelty labels: "reproduction", "known idea" (cite it), "known ideas, new combination", or "new". "New" needs a prior-art search covering at least the 2023-2025 BabyLM proceedings plus a general search, which the critic checks. Unknown = "known idea" by default.
- Curves: every run saves its loss curve as CSV plus PNG.
