# Known ideas: measured data efficiency under a fixed, repeated data budget (priors for BabyLM 10M x 10 epochs)

Compiled 2026-10-09. Numbers are quoted from the PDFs (downloaded from arXiv or ACL Anthology, pdftotext). "my calc" = arithmetic I did on the published numbers. UNVERIFIED = not checked against the primary text.
Our target regime is roughly 13M tokens (10M words), at most 10 epochs (the BabyLM 2025 rule is "at most 100M words" of exposure for strict-small), 30M–120M params. That is 2–9 params per unique token, so these models are over-parameterized, as in Kim et al.

## Notes (priors for attacks)
1. **Not found (searched, nothing quantitative):** checkpoint averaging/EMA, label smoothing, nGPT or Muon under multi-epoch fixed data, and kNN-LM/retrieval on BabyLM. Label smoothing appears only in Xue (62.6, below dropout's 62.9). A 2024 BabyLM self/born-again distillation paper reports "consistent and modest" gains, numbers UNVERIFIED (search snippet only).
2. **The 10-epoch cap means we are mostly not in the overfitting regime.** By Muennighoff's law, 10 epochs is worth about 7.8× the unique data. Kim et al.'s optimum used 8–16 epochs, and the diffusion-vs-AR crossover sits above BabyLM compute. Methods that pay off only after hundreds of epochs (diffusion's R*=513) buy little here. Bottlenecks at 10 epochs are more likely optimization or under-training plus regularization than memorization. Our Phase-2 measurements should check this.
3. **Biggest verified multipliers in the same family as ours:** regularization (2.29×), joint parameter + ensemble scaling (5.17×, inference-heavy), and distilling those into a small student (keeps 83%). Baby Llama shows the distillation direction already works on BabyLM 10M (+3.7 BLiMP). Teachers must be trained only on the 10M words. Whether synthetic or teacher tokens count against the 100M-word exposure cap needs a rules check (UNVERIFIED). Logit distillation (Baby Llama) adds no new words, but sampling text (Kim) may count.
4. The AR–diffusion gap shrinks to about zero once AR gets strong weight decay (Xu 2026). The objective-level wins on BabyLM (MNTP hybrid, adaptive masking, MTP) are each worth ≤1–4 pp on one benchmark and about 1 pp on the aggregate.
5. Methods that need an external LLM (WRAP, EntiGraph, simplification) violate the strict tracks, so they can only serve as labeled reference points.
