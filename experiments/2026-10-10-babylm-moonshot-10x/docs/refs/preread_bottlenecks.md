# Pre-read: provisional top 3 bottlenecks (published 2025 baselines)

2026-10-09, instruments thread. Pre-read on the published BabyLM 2025 checkpoints, not on our reproduced winner; the research experimenter owns the final ranking. Dev sample of 138,745 words from the official dev split (no eval items), each model scored in its own training text format; GPT-BERT 10M gives 3.109 nats/token here vs 3.10 on its model card. Gap = 10M final minus 100M final, nats/word, 95% chunk-bootstrap CI. Figures and tables: `results/babylm/preread_v1/` (start with `prize_anatomy.png`).

**The prize.** GPT-BERT causal 0.844 [0.790, 0.908] (5.068 vs 4.225); GPT-2 1.076 [1.003, 1.158] (5.481 vs 4.405). GPT-BERT 10M is 0.413 better than GPT-2 10M. Reweighted to the official dev split's source word shares (critic ref-0255; the sample under-weights simple_wiki): 0.876 [0.824, 0.933] and 1.128 [1.060, 1.202]. Speaker labels (`*CHI:`, `A:`; 5.4% of words) carry 1.3% and 2.1% of the gap; per word without them 0.880 and 1.114.

**Ruled out as main bottlenecks (both families):** long context (the gap is flat from the 4th word to the end of 360-word chunks); global calibration (best single temperature worth 0.013 and 0.000); verbatim memorisation. In-context copying is a GPT-2-only weakness: the gap on repeated words exceeds first occurrences by 0.065 for GPT-2 but by -0.064 for GPT-BERT.

**Confounds.** The GPT-2 baselines train at lr 5e-5 (64% of their gap already shows on seen text, so part is under-training). GPT-BERT 100M has 120M parameters vs 31M at 10M and an 8x larger batch, so part of its seen-text gap is size. The count model and cache predict the word but not the whitespace before it, so their gains are slightly optimistic.

**Lever signatures** (`signature.png`, `signature_tilts.csv`). Share of the weaker model's loss each lever removes, as two tilts (95% CI): rare = words seen 1 to 15 times vs 64+ times; novelty = trigram in neither corpus vs in train_10M. 10x data: rare 1.83 [1.68, 1.99] and 2.31, novelty 1.04 [1.00, 1.10] and 1.05. Extra epochs: novelty 0.43 to 0.76, always under 1. Fresh vs repeated text (GPT-2, equal steps): rare 3.37, novelty 1.64. The GPT-2 to GPT-BERT recipe change: rare 3.48, novelty 0.96, so on novelty it looks like data, not like more passes. An attack that sits near the 10x-data point is buying what data buys; one with novelty under 1 is buying what epochs buy.

**Pre-registered predictions** (`preread_hypotheses.md`): H1 not confirmed (words seen 16 to 1023 times carry 33 to 34%, predicted at least 40%). H2 confirmed (trigrams in neither corpus 42 to 43%, only in 100M 20 to 21%). H3 confirmed at 10 epochs. H4 confirmed in both families (6% and 2.4% of the gain after epoch 7, predicted under 10%; dev minus train grows every epoch). H5 mixed (GPT-2 copy lag 6% of the gap, above the predicted 5%; GPT-BERT has none). H6 mixed (GPT-BERT best T 1.1 worth 0.013; GPT-2 best T 1.0). H7 mixed (bursty words 1.29x [1.21, 1.37] for GPT-BERT, 1.15x for GPT-2, predicted at least 1.2x in both).
